隱私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 1 日

適用範圍與聯絡方式

本政策說明 YanYan（妍妍冒險傳）iOS 遊戲如何處理資料，包括 TestFlight 測試版。開發者為 Vic Lin，聯絡信箱為 viclinviclin@gmail.com。

遊戲存檔與本機紀錄

目前遊戲不要求建立帳號。進度、裝備、金幣、設定與復活次數儲存在裝置上；開發者目前沒有提供雲端存檔伺服器。若開啟本機測試紀錄，相關遊玩事件也只保存在本機，可在遊戲提供的紀錄選項中清除。這些本機紀錄不會由遊戲傳送給開發者。

廣告與 Google 服務

遊戲整合 Google AdMob 與 Google User Messaging Platform，提供自願觀看的獎勵廣告及廣告隱私選擇。測試建置使用 Google 測試廣告；正式廣告是否啟用，依發布版本而定。Google SDK 可能處理 IP 位址推估的概略位置、裝置識別資訊、廣告曝光與互動、效能和當機診斷資料，用於廣告、分析及防止詐欺。非個人化廣告不代表完全不處理資料。

隱私選擇與裝置權限

目前遊戲程式請求非個人化廣告，不主動要求跨 App 追蹤授權，也不要求相機、麥克風、通訊錄或精確 GPS 位置權限。法律要求同意的地區，廣告服務需先完成適用的隱私選擇。可在「隱私與支援 → 廣告隱私選擇」使用 Google 提供的選項（依地區及服務判定顯示）。您也可以不觀看獎勵廣告，免費返回村莊繼續遊玩。

客服電子郵件

如果您寄信給客服，我們會收到您的信箱地址、訊息及您主動提供的截圖或其他附件，只用於回覆、調查問題及必要的客服紀錄。請勿寄送密碼、付款卡號、Apple 金鑰或其他敏感資訊。客服信件由 Gmail 處理。

第三方、跨境處理與網站

Google 廣告服務與 Gmail 可能在您所在國家以外處理資料，並依 Google 的隱私與資料保護規則運作。請參閱 Google 隱私政策及 Google 廣告資料說明。本支援網站沒有自行加入分析追蹤或廣告腳本；網站託管服務仍可能處理 IP 與請求紀錄以提供及保護網站。若透過 GitHub Pages 訪問，亦適用 GitHub 隱私聲明。

保留、刪除與您的權利

本機存檔會保留到您清除遊戲資料或刪除 App；僅「卸載 App」可能保留資料，系統備份亦依裝置設定管理。刪除前請留意可能失去遊戲進度。客服資料僅於處理問題、後續追蹤及必要紀錄期間保留，不再需要時刪除，法律要求保留者除外。Google 管理的廣告資料依其保留規則處理，並非開發者可直接從本機存檔刪除。依適用法律，您可要求查閱、更正、刪除資料或撤回同意，請使用客服信箱聯絡；必要時我們會合理確認身分並協助轉介第三方服務。

兒童與監護人

未成年玩家應遵守所在地的年齡與監護人同意規定。請勿在客服訊息提供兒童的敏感個人資料。若監護人認為兒童資料被不當提供或處理，請聯絡我們協助處理；廣告是否可提供仍需符合適用的年齡與同意要求。

政策更新

遊戲新增或調整資料處理方式時，我們會更新此頁及更新日期；依法需重新取得同意時，會先取得適用同意。

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Scope and contact

This policy explains data handling in YanYan for iOS, including its TestFlight builds. The developer is Vic Lin. Contact: viclinviclin@gmail.com.

Game saves and local records

The current game does not require an account. Progress, equipment, coins, settings and revival limits are saved on your device; the developer does not currently operate a cloud-save server. Optional local testing records remain on the device and can be cleared using the game’s record controls. The game does not send these local records to the developer.

Advertising and Google services

The game integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and Google User Messaging Platform for advertising privacy choices. Test builds use Google test ads; live advertising depends on the released build. Google’s SDK may process IP-based approximate location, device identifiers, ad views and interactions, performance information and crash diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing.

Privacy choices and device permissions

The current game code requests non-personalized ads, does not initiate cross-app tracking permission requests, and does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise GPS access. Where consent is legally required, applicable advertising privacy choices must be completed first. Google’s available choices can be accessed through Privacy & Support → Ad privacy choices, depending on region and service requirements. You may decline rewarded ads and return to the village for free to continue playing.

Support emails

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any screenshots or attachments you choose to provide. We use these to respond, investigate issues and maintain necessary support records. Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, Apple keys or other sensitive information. Support email is handled through Gmail.

Third parties, international processing and this website

Google advertising services and Gmail may process data outside your country under Google’s privacy and data-protection rules. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising information. This support website adds no analytics tracking or advertising scripts. Its hosting provider may still process IP addresses and request logs to deliver and protect the site. When accessed through GitHub Pages, the GitHub Privacy Statement also applies.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local saves remain until game data is cleared or the app is deleted. Offloading the app may retain data, and system backups depend on device settings. Deletion may erase your progress. Support information is retained only while needed for the issue, follow-up and necessary records, unless law requires longer retention. Google-managed advertising data follows Google’s retention rules and cannot be deleted by clearing a game save. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent by emailing support. We may reasonably verify identity and help direct requests to the relevant third-party service.

Children and guardians

Minors should follow local age and guardian-consent requirements. Do not include children’s sensitive personal information in support messages. Guardians who believe a child’s data has been improperly provided or processed should contact us for assistance. Advertising must meet applicable age and consent requirements.

Policy updates

We will update this page and its date when the game’s data practices change. Where renewed consent is legally required, applicable consent will be obtained first.
