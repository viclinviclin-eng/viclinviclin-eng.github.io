寄送客服信件

回報問題時請附上

  • iPhone 型號與 iOS 版本。
  • 遊戲版本與建置號（可在 TestFlight 查看）。
  • 發生問題的關卡，以及問題發生前的操作。
  • 如果方便，附上截圖或短片。

請遮住截圖中的私人資訊，不需提供密碼、銀行資料或開發者金鑰。

廣告沒有載入？

先確認網路連線，稍後重試。您可以選擇免費回村繼續冒險。未完成獎勵條件不會給予復活，也不扣除觀看復活次數；若結果與此不同，請附上版本及發生時間回報。

存檔與隱私

目前進度存於手機本機。處理問題前請保留 App；刪除 App 可能遺失進度。資料處理方式與刪除請求請參閱 隱私政策。

Player support

Email the developer for game issues, control questions or privacy requests.

Contact Vic Lin

viclinviclin@gmail.com

Email support

Include these details

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The game version and build number, available in TestFlight.
  • The area affected and what you did before the issue.
  • A screenshot or short recording, if convenient.

Hide private information in screenshots. Passwords, bank details and developer keys are not needed.

Ad not loading?

Check your connection and try again later. You can return to the village for free and keep playing. Without completing the reward requirements, no revival is granted and no ad-revival use is deducted. If you experience different behavior, report the version and time.

Saves and privacy

Progress is currently stored on your phone. Keep the app installed while troubleshooting; deleting it may erase progress. See the privacy policy for data practices and deletion requests.
